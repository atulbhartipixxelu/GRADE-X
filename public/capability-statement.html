Grade X operates a stainless tracked crawler for kitchen exhaust interiors — dual-hose high-pressure cleaning, twin turret nozzles, LED lighting and a forward inspection camera.

  1. Stainless tracked crawler

    Chevron-plate tracks and a sealed stainless chassis so the unit can drive grease-slick duct floors instead of sending a person inside.

  2. Twin turret nozzles

    An articulating head with two forward jets, fed by dual high-pressure hoses and brass quick-connects — the same layout as this machine.

  3. Inspection camera + LED

  4. Exploded service architecture

    Drive motors, track assemblies, rollers and turret are built to be serviced. The build matches this platform, not a generic robot.

Grade XMethodology | Page 06

How Grade X
actually operates.

A dedicated numbered sequence — evidence of a repeatable, professional process, not folded into general service descriptions.

  1. 01

    Site inspection and assessment

    The system is walked, photographed and scoped before any cleaning starts so the method matches the site.

  2. 02

    Grease thickness measurement

    A digital grease thickness gauge — including the Teinnova Grasmeter — captures real-time micron readings.

  3. 03

    Preparation and protection

    Cookline, storage and guest-adjacent zones are isolated and protected. Live kitchens stay on where agreed.

  4. 04

    Interior steam washing

    Professional-grade steam and extraction lift grease inside the exhaust system, with less harsh chemistry.

  5. 05

    Canopy, ductwork and components

    Canopy interiors, filters and accessible ductwork are cleaned as one path. Crawler used where manned entry is unsafe.

  6. 06

    Final inspection and quality control

    The completed system is inspected against the original scope. Missed soil is treated as a defect.

  7. 07

    Post-cleaning grease measurement

    The same digital method after the clean. Before-and-after micron readings sit in the job file.

  8. 08

    Reporting and documentation

    Before/after photos, measurement records, compliance notes and next-interval recommendations.
